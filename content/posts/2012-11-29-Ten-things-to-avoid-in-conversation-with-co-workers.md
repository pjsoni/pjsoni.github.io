summary: "A short collection of things that are better left unsaid in the workplace. The examples cover personal questions, gossip, criticism, assumptions, and other comments that can easily create uncomfortable or unproductive conversations with coworkers."
---

Here are 10 things you should never say at work.

- “Are you pregnant?”
- “You owe $10 for this gift for the boss.”
- “You’re so skinny! Why aren’t you eating?”
- “That’s not my job.”
- “The new manager is a real jerk.”
- “I heard Kim is dating Ryan.”
- “You’re HOW old?”
- “Don’t ask me. They don’t tell me anything.”
- “Why are you so dressed up today? Got a job interview?”
- “I’m so hungover.”
